# Outreach — get your first clients

Goal: **book free audits.** Target ~30–50 touches/day. Track everything in a sheet
(Business · Contact · Channel · Date · Status · Next step).

## Who to target first
- Local service businesses with visible "reply lag": salons, clinics, gyms, dentists, trades, real estate, restaurants.
- Online stores (Shopify/Etsy/IG shops) that answer DMs slowly.
- **Signal to look for:** they post often, get comments/DMs, but reply slowly or have an unanswered inbox. That's your wedge.

## The rule
Lead with **their pain + a specific win**, not "I do AI." Offer the free audit. Soft close.

## The free audit call (15 min) — script
1. **Ask (5 min):** "Walk me through what happens when a new customer messages you."
   Find the time-drain and the leak (missed messages = lost money).
2. **Diagnose (3 min):** name the 1–2 highest-value automations. Keep it simple.
3. **Show (4 min):** share screen, demo your pre-built example doing exactly that.
4. **Close (3 min):** "I can build this for [Business] in about a week for [$price].
   I take a 50% deposit to start. Want me to send a one-pager?"
5. **Always** send the proposal within the hour while it's hot.

## Objection handling
- *"Too expensive."* → "It pays for itself the first time it saves a missed booking. And there's a results guarantee."
- *"I'm not techy."* → "That's the point — I handle everything, you just enjoy the results."
- *"Will it sound robotic?"* → "It's built in your voice and reviewed with you. I'll show you."
- *"Let me think."* → "Totally. Want me to send the 60-sec demo + one-pager so you can decide?"

## Targets
- 50 DMs/day → ~5–10 replies → ~2–3 audits booked.
- ~1 in 3 audits closes once you have a clean demo. That's your first clients.
