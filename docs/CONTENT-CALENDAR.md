# Content — 30-day plan + Reel/Short scripts

Post **1 short video/day** (Reels + Shorts + TikTok, same clip). Format that wins:
**Problem → Demo → Result**, 15–30s, screen recording, big captions, trending audio.
Hashtags: `#AIAutomation #SmallBizAI #AItools #automation #[yourtown]`.

## The repeatable formula (every video)
1. **Hook (0–3s):** a pain or bold claim on screen. ("This replaced 10 hrs of replies.")
2. **Demo (3–20s):** screen-record the automation doing the thing.
3. **Result (20–27s):** the outcome. ("Booked, while she slept.")
4. **CTA (last 3s):** "Free AI audit — link in bio."

## Sample scripts

**Reel 1 — Booking bot**

- Demo: type "Hi do you have Sat 2pm for a cut?" → bot: "Yes! Booked you in ✅"
- Result text: "She booked at 11pm. You did nothing."
- VO/caption end: "I build these for local businesses. Free audit, link in bio."

- Result: "Faster reply = the job is yours."
- CTA: "Free AI audit — link in bio."

**Reel 9 — Explainer**
- Hook: "An AI receptionist, explained in 60 seconds"

## Rules that matter
- **Consistency > perfection.** 30 mediocre posts beat 3 perfect ones.
- **Re-use everything:** one demo recording → a Reel, a DM asset, a website clip.
- **Always end with the same CTA** so the funnel is obvious.
- Repurpose top performers monthly.
