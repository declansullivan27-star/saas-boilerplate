# Delivery — how to build the 5 automations you sell

Learn these on a demo business first, then deliver them to clients. For each: the
tools, the build, the demo to record, and what to charge.

> **Default AI:** use the latest Claude models (e.g. Claude Opus 4.x) for quality,
> or a cheaper/faster model for high-volume replies. Most no-code tools let you pick.

---

## 1. AI Reception Chatbot — $500–$1,500
**Answers FAQs, books appointments, captures leads 24/7 on site + Instagram + WhatsApp.**

- **Tools:** a chatbot builder (e.g. Voiceflow, Chatbase, Botpress, or a custom GPT) + the client's calendar (Cal.com) + their FAQ content.
- **Build:**
  1. Gather their FAQs, services, prices, hours, policies into one doc.
  2. Create the bot, feed it that doc as its knowledge base.
  3. Set the persona/voice (friendly, on-brand). Add a booking handoff that drops people into their Cal.com link.
  4. Add lead capture (name + contact) when the bot can't fully answer.
  5. Embed on their site (script tag) and connect Instagram/WhatsApp DMs if needed.
- **Demo to record:** a customer asking hours + price, then booking — all by chat.
- **Watch-outs:** keep the knowledge base small and accurate; test the 10 most common questions.

## 2. Lead Responder & Qualifier — $500–$2,000
**Instantly replies to new enquiries, asks qualifying questions, routes the good ones.**

- **Tools:** Make.com or Zapier (the automation glue) + their lead source (web form, IG, email) + AI step + their CRM/calendar/Slack.
- **Build:**
  1. Trigger: new lead (form submit, new IG DM, new email).
  2. AI step: draft a reply + extract budget/intent/timeline.
  3. Logic: if qualified → notify the owner + offer booking link; if not → polite nurture.
  4. Log every lead to a sheet/CRM so nothing leaks.
- **Demo to record:** submit a fake enquiry → watch an on-brand reply land in under a minute.

## 3. Content & Reply Engine — $300–$1,500 (often a retainer)
**Generates captions, product descriptions, SEO copy and customer replies on tap.**

- **Tools:** Claude/ChatGPT + saved prompt templates; optionally a Make/Zapier flow or a simple custom GPT the client can use themselves.
- **Build:**
  1. Write tight, reusable prompts for each output (caption, product desc, review reply).
  2. Bake in their brand voice + examples.
  3. Package as a custom GPT or a Notion/Sheet of prompts, or a button-based Make scenario.
- **Demo to record:** paste a product name → get 5 on-brand descriptions instantly.

## 4. Workflow Automation — $500–$3,000
**Connects tools so admin, follow-ups and data move on their own.**

- **Tools:** Make.com / Zapier + whatever they already use (Sheets, QuickBooks, Shopify, email, calendar).
- **Common wins:** appointment reminders, review requests after a job, invoice follow-ups, order/inventory sync, weekly report emails.
- **Build:** map the manual process → replace each step with a trigger/action → test with real data → add error notifications.
- **Demo to record:** a job marked "done" auto-sends a review request + logs it.

## 5. Retainer / Maintenance — $200–$1,000/mo
**Monitoring, tuning, small additions. Your recurring revenue base.**

- Monthly: review chatbot transcripts, fix gaps, add new automations, send a short results summary.
- This is where the business becomes stable income — pitch it on every project.

---

## Universal delivery SOP
1. **Audit call** → identify the single highest-value automation. Don't oversell.
2. **One-page proposal** → outcome, scope, price, timeline (see OPERATIONS.md).
3. **50% deposit** before you start.
4. **Build in a copy/sandbox**, not their live account where possible.
5. **Review call** → walk them through it, get sign-off.
6. **Go live + balance invoice.**
7. **Record a before/after** for content (with permission) and pitch the retainer.
8. **Ask for a testimonial** 1–2 weeks later once they've felt the time saved.
