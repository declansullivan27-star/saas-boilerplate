# Operations — pricing, payments, contracts, tools

## Pricing (matches the site)
| Tier | Price | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $500+ | One automation, ~1 week |
| Growth | $2,000+ | A full system (chatbot + responder + follow-ups) |
| Retainer | $200–$1,000/mo | Monitoring, tuning, new automations |

**Rules of thumb**
- Price on **value, not hours** (saving 10 hrs/week is worth far more than your time to build it).
- Always take a **50% deposit** before starting.
- **Pitch the retainer on every project** — recurring revenue is the goal.
- Raise prices once you have 2–3 case studies.

## Payments — Stripe
1. Create a Stripe account.
2. Use **Payment Links** or **Invoices** for deposits + balances (no code needed).
3. For retainers, create a **subscription** product and send the checkout link.
4. Keep a simple income/expenses sheet from day one (tax matters).

## The one-page proposal (send within an hour of the audit)
```
[Business] — AI Automation Proposal

Goal:        [the measurable outcome, e.g. "reply to every enquiry in <1 min, 24/7"]
What I'll build:
  - [automation 1]
  - [automation 2]
Timeline:    ~[X] days
Investment:  $[price]  (50% to start, 50% on delivery)
Guarantee:   If it doesn't [agreed outcome], I'll fix it or refund.

To start: reply "yes" and I'll send the deposit link.
```

## Client onboarding checklist
- ☐ Deposit paid
- ☐ Access granted (calendar, IG/site, tools, brand voice + FAQ doc)
- ☐ Agreed outcome written down (this is your guarantee benchmark)
- ☐ Build in sandbox → review call → go live
- ☐ Balance invoice paid
- ☐ Recorded a before/after (with permission)
- ☐ Asked for testimonial + pitched retainer

| Demos/design | Canva, Loom/your phone for screen recordings |

## Trust & safety (do these)
- Only connect accounts the client explicitly approves; build in sandboxes first.
- Don't store customer data you don't need; respect the Privacy Policy on the site.
- Be honest about what AI can/can't do. Under-promise, over-deliver.
- Keep the results guarantee real — it's your biggest closer with no track record.

## What to do in your first week (summary)
1. Finish RUNBOOK.md (site live, tools ready).
2. Build 2–3 demos from DELIVERY.md and record them.
3. Build a 50-business outreach list.
4. Start OUTREACH.md (30–50/day) + post daily from CONTENT-CALENDAR.md.
5. Book audits → send proposals same day → close → deliver → get testimonial → repeat.
